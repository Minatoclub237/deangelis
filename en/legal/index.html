Privacy

Who is responsible for your data?

The salon, using the contact details above.

What the website stores

Nothing. The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising, and its fonts and media are served from its own hosting. That is why no consent is requested when you arrive.

Appointment requests and the contact form

These forms send nothing to the website: they prepare an email in your email app. The salon only receives your information if you send that email: name, phone, email, services, preferred date and time, message.

It is used only to answer your request and organise your appointment, at your request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). It is read by the salon and passes through its email services, including Google for the Gmail address. The salon keeps it for at most to be completed by the salon.
